# CreatorPulse – Influencer Performance & Sustainable Customer Acquisition Analytics Platform

## Project Description
CreatorPulse is an analytics platform designed to evaluate influencer campaign metrics, referral traffic, and customer purchase behavior to help marketing teams discover sustainable customer acquisition patterns.

## Problem Statement
A social commerce platform captures influencer campaign metrics, referral traffic, and purchase behaviour, but marketing teams still cannot determine which creator engagement patterns produce sustainable customer acquisition.

## Project Goal
To empower marketing teams with data-driven insights and analytics to identify high-converting creator engagement patterns that drive long-term, sustainable customer growth.

## Planned Technology Stack
- React.js
- Node.js
- Express.js
- MongoDB
- Tailwind CSS
- JWT
- Recharts
